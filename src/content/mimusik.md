MiMusik was a product for requesting personalized music. Its bilingual interface used React 18, Vite and Tailwind CSS. Visitors could listen to examples with an audio player and start an order conversation through WhatsApp.

## How the experience worked

The site brought product information and audio examples together in a bilingual interface. WhatsApp handled order intake through a conversation. The browser demo below illustrates the kind of information a request could contain.
